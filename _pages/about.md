<div class="intro">
<p>I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Michigan and a Graduate Fellow in the Demography Training Program at the <a href="https://psc.isr.umich.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Population Studies Center</a>. My research agenda focuses on topics at the intersection of health, development, and digital economics.</p>

<p>I previously served as a predoctoral research fellow at the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research (SIEPR) with Professor <a href="https://www.matthewgentzkow.com/bio/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Matthew Gentzkow</a>, and as a full-time research assistant at the Brown University School of Public Health with Dean <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashish_Jha" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ashish Jha</a>. I earned my BA at the University of Southern California, where I studied Economics and Political Science.</p>
